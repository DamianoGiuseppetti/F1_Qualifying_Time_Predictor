# Data Quirks & Assumptions

_Auto-generated by scripts/validate_schema.py - re-run after every download batch._

A meaningful share of laps in every session legitimately have no `LapTime` - out-laps, in-laps, and laps cut short by a red flag or safety car never complete a timed lap. Rather than a fixed bar, each session type's normal range is computed from this dataset (Tukey's rule: flag past Q3 + 1.5×IQR) and only genuine outliers are listed below.
